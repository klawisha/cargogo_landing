FIELD NOTE / 07

The curious story
behind “cargo cult”.

“Cargo cult” is an anthropological label associated with several Melanesian movements of the 20th century. Contact with colonial systems, military bases and huge flows of unfamiliar goods sometimes became part of new ritual and political movements.

The famous image of improvised runways, towers or aircraft built to make the cargo return is a popular shorthand — memorable, but much simpler than the real histories behind those movements.
